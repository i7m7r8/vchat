Vchat is a peer-to-peer encrypted messenger that routes all traffic through the Tor network.

Every peer runs an embedded Tor hidden service (pure-Rust Arti), so there are no servers, no tracking and no central collection of metadata.

Security
- End-to-end encryption with AES-256-GCM
- X25519 key exchange over a Noise XX handshake
- Ed25519 signatures on every message
- Forward secrecy with ephemeral per-session keys
- Your onion address is your identity, no registration needed

Features
- Text messaging with delivery/read receipts and typing indicators
- Group messaging, reactions and disappearing messages
- Voice and video calls, and screen sharing
- File transfer and QR-code contact exchange
- Embedded Tor: no external daemon, no special permissions
- Dark, privacy-first UI

Vchat is 100% open source (GPL-3.0-only) and collects no data.
