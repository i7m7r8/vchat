Vchat is a peer-to-peer encrypted messenger that routes all traffic through the Tor network.

Features:
- End-to-end encrypted messaging with AES-256-GCM
- X25519 key exchange with Noise Protocol
- Tor hidden service for private connections
- Group messaging
- Message reactions
- Disappearing messages with configurable TTL
- QR code contact sharing
- Dark theme UI
- Open source and privacy-respecting
